The First Table was the first Infinae edition.

Hosted in a private dining room at Four Seasons Hotel The Westcliff, the afternoon
brought together a small group of guests for caviar, wine, fine dining and a guided
conversation around confidence and ease at the table.

Rather than separating learning from the dining experience, guests explored formal
dining while experiencing it around the table.

The afternoon included:

- a welcome drink
- a guided dining session
- a caviar lesson and tasting
- a three course lunch with wine and non-alcoholic pairings
- a closing gift for each guest
